BIGGEST GAPS BETWEEN TEAMS
🏎️
F1: Ad Sales way ahead of AM
Ad Sales scores F1 at 1.78 vs AM at 3.20. Sellers are getting direct requests from buyers. AMs note there are no commercial breaks during races, which complicates delivery.
🥊
MMA/UFC: Polarizing but real potential
3 respondents rated it a 1 ("huge potential"), but 4 rated it a 5. Revenue potential is concentrated in nicotine, gambling, and alcohol verticals. Other brands avoid fighting sports entirely.
🏀
WNBA: Stronger on the sales side
Ad Sales rates WNBA at 2.44 vs AM at 3.00. Women's sports in general are highly requested by advertisers, though bar viewership for women's leagues is still developing.
